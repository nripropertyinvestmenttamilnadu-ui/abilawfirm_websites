Title Verification & Legal Opinion in Chennai

Before you buy land, a flat or a house, or pledge property for a loan, our advocates check the complete chain of title and give you a clear written legal opinion, so you know the property is safe to deal with.

What We Verify

  • Title deeds and the full chain of parent documents
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for mortgages and other charges
  • Patta, chitta, adangal and FMB sketch
  • Seller's identity, ownership and right to sell
  • Legal heirship, succession and partition history
  • Power of attorney validity, if the sale is through an agent
  • Layout and building approvals (DTCP / CMDA) and plan sanction
  • Pending litigation, attachments and court orders

Documents Required

  • Copy of the current sale deed or title deed
  • Parent / link documents for as many years as available
  • Latest Encumbrance Certificate
  • Patta, chitta and tax receipts
  • Approved layout or building plan, if any
  • Legal heir certificate or death certificate, where applicable
  • Seller's ID proof

Don't have everything? Send us what you have and we'll tell you what's missing.

Our Process

  1. Share DocumentsSend copies of the property documents in person, by email or on WhatsApp.
  2. Document ScrutinyOur advocates examine each document and trace the chain of title.
  3. Records CheckWe check the EC, revenue records and approvals, and look for pending cases.
  4. ClarificationsWe list any gaps or defects and explain how they can be fixed.
  5. Legal OpinionYou receive a signed written opinion on whether the title is clear and marketable.

Who Needs It

  • Buyers of land, plots, flats and independent houses
  • NRIs buying property in Chennai and Tamil Nadu
  • Banks and borrowers needing a legal opinion for a home loan or loan against property
  • Families dealing with ancestral or inherited property
  • Builders and developers going into joint development

What You Receive

  • A signed, written legal opinion on the title
  • A list of documents examined
  • Any defects found and the steps to cure them
  • Advice on the conditions to put in your sale agreement
  • A consultation with the advocate to go through the findings

Frequently Asked Questions

How many years of documents should be verified?

We usually trace the title for at least 30 years, and further back where the history needs it, so that ownership is traced back to a clear source.

How long does title verification take?

It depends on how many documents there are and how complex the history is. We'll give you a timeline once we've seen your documents.

Can you verify property if I live abroad?

Yes. You can send documents by email or WhatsApp, and we'll share the legal opinion digitally and go through it with you by phone or video call.

Is a bank's legal opinion enough?

A bank's opinion protects the bank's loan. An independent opinion from your own advocate protects you as the buyer and covers what matters to you.

Registration Services in Chennai

We draft your deed, guide you on stamp duty and registration fees, and handle registration at the Sub-Registrar office from start to finish, so your document is correct and legally valid.

Documents We Register

  • Sale deeds
  • Gift deeds
  • Settlement deeds
  • Partition deeds
  • Release deeds
  • Wills
  • General & special power of attorney
  • Sale agreements
  • Lease and rental agreements
  • Mortgage deeds
  • Rectification & cancellation deeds
  • Joint development agreements

Documents Required

  • Parent documents of the property
  • Latest Encumbrance Certificate and patta
  • ID and address proof of all parties (Aadhaar, PAN)
  • Passport-size photographs of all parties
  • Details of two witnesses with ID proof
  • Power of attorney, if anyone is signing for another person

The exact list depends on the type of document. We confirm it with you before the registration date.

Who It's For

  • Buyers and sellers of property
  • Families transferring property by gift, settlement or partition
  • People making a will
  • NRIs who need a power of attorney to act in India
  • Landlords and tenants
  • Builders and landowners

Our Process

  1. ConsultationWe understand the transaction and check the property documents.
  2. DraftingOur advocates draft the deed and share it with you for review.
  3. Stamp Duty & FeesWe work out the guideline value, stamp duty and registration fees.
  4. Slot BookingWe prepare the online application and book your appointment at the Sub-Registrar office.
  5. Registration DayWe attend with you at the Sub-Registrar office and see the registration through.
  6. CollectionWe help you collect the registered document and advise on the patta transfer.

Why Use an Advocate for Registration

  • Deeds drafted correctly, avoiding costly mistakes later
  • Title checked before you register
  • Correct stamp duty, so there are no penalties or objections
  • Smooth handling at the Sub-Registrar office
  • Clear advice on tax and legal consequences
  • Help with the patta transfer after registration

Frequently Asked Questions

Do all parties need to be present for registration?

Usually all executing parties and two witnesses must be present at the Sub-Registrar office. If someone can't attend, they can act through a registered power of attorney.

How is stamp duty calculated?

Stamp duty and registration fees depend on the type of document and the property's value, which is generally the guideline value or the deal value, whichever is higher. We'll tell you the exact amount for your document.

Does a will have to be registered?

Registering a will is not compulsory, but it is strongly recommended because it makes the will much harder to challenge later.

Can an NRI register a document without coming to India?

Yes. An NRI can execute a power of attorney abroad, attested as the law requires, so that a trusted person can register the document in India. We'll guide you through the steps.
